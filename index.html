Your hands are the controller

Open palm

The swarm floats. Wave your hand and random balls drift the way you move.

Point

Stream the swarm where your finger points, head first, like a comet.

Two fingers

Make the swarm circle. A closed fist lets everything drop.

Pinch

Pull the balls into a tight clump that hits much harder.

Both hands open

Stop: the swarm brakes to a standstill.

Up and running in a minute

  1. Download and install
    macOS: open the .dmg and drag Telekinesis to Applications. Windows: run the installer.
  2. Open it and press Home → How to play
    A short guided lesson teaches each gesture by doing it.
  3. Allow the camera and hold a hand up
    About an arm’s length from the screen, in good light. No camera? Use the mouse button in the top bar.

Good to know

macOS says “unidentified developer”

The app is not signed with a paid Apple certificate yet. Right-click (or Ctrl-click) Telekinesis in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.

Windows shows “protected your PC”

SmartScreen warns about apps without a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Is my camera recorded?

No. Hand tracking runs on your computer; the video is never saved or sent anywhere, and the game works offline.

What do I need?

macOS 11 or newer on an Apple Silicon Mac, or 64-bit Windows 10/11 with Microsoft Edge WebView2 (preinstalled on current Windows), and a webcam. Intel Macs: see all downloads below.

No camera? Play with the mouse

Press the mouse button in the top bar. Hold the mouse button = open palm, arrow keys = point, Shift = two fingers, Alt = pinch, Space = both hands open.
